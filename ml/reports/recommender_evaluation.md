# Recommendation engine evaluation

Engine `weighted-v1`, 15 scripted scenarios, 275 approved **synthetic** listings, top-5. Generated by `scripts/evaluate_recommender.py`; every number below comes from that run.

> There are no genuine relevance labels, so no Precision@K is reported. "Ideal" listings are defined by rules written from the specification (right type and legal status for the purpose, inside budget and preferred area, known and usable road access, at least 70% documentation declared). Agreement with those rules shows the engine does what it was designed to do, not that buyers would agree with it.

## Summary

- Constraint violations (independent checker, all scenarios): **0**
- Mean pairwise agreement with rule-derived expectations (partly by construction, since both follow the same specification): **99%** over 971 ideal/non-ideal pairs
- Mean Kendall's tau under ±20% random weight changes: **0.94** (1.0 = order unchanged)

| Top-5, mean over scenarios | Weighted engine | Cheapest first | Newest first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Available ideal listings that reached the top 5 | 100% | 0% | 13% |
| Share of top 5 that is rule-derived ideal (capped by how many exist) | 31% | 0% | 3% |
| Documentation completeness | 76% | 61% | 58% |
| Road access known | 100% | 96% | 94% |
| Purpose suitability (engine's own table, so partly circular) | 0.86 | 0.76 | 0.68 |

The baselines rank the same listings that pass the strict filters, so they never violate constraints either; the comparison is about what rises to the top.

## Notes

- **KNN towards a preference vector** (a pseudo-listing at 80% of the budget, the preferred type, area and location) has no notion of strict requirements, so it can return listings that break them. That is why KNN is used only for "similar listings" on a property page, and the weighted engine for the Advisor.
- **Stability:** weights are design assumptions; the tau figures show how much the order depends on them.
- *₹2 lakh plot (expected: no results)*: no results; exclusions were {'Over your maximum budget': 275}. The Advisor reports these counts so the user knows which requirement to relax.
