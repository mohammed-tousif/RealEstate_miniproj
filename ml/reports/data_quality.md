## Rules applied
- Schema and enum validation; rows with invalid values are excluded.
- Areas converted to sq ft (1 acre = 43,560, 1 gunta = 1,089, 1 sq m = 10.7639104).
- Coordinates outside the Karnataka bounding box are treated as missing.
- Listings with status *rejected* or *archived* are excluded (not valid asking prices).
- Near-duplicates (same locality and type, area and price within 2%, coordinates within 100 m) are grouped; one copy is kept so a re-listing cannot appear in both training and test data.
- Price outliers: log(₹/sq ft) is compared with the median of similar listings (same locality and type, or same type when fewer than 3). Beyond 1.5 × IQR is flagged and kept; more than 5× away (typo-like) is excluded from training. Nothing else is removed.
- Consistency: width × length vs stated area (10% tolerance), agricultural classification with conversion claimed, unknown road access. These are flagged and kept.
